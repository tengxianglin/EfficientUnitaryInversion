# Complement-folded Plucker fusion frames for the EUI moment target

Date: 2026-11-28. Run 66. Base inspected: main at 15832cb21b9ec601bf8e1dc35c1a3997e59d1341.

## Scope and statement

This note gives an exact geometric reformulation of the full balanced-slice program-mass condition and a polynomial-time-per-sample falsification statistic. It does **not** construct an N=42 ensemble or prove a Choi/diamond bound.

Let N=2n and let Q(c)=R(c)D be the balanced symmetric involution associated with a legal even-cycle seed, so Q=Q^T, Q^2=I and tr Q=0. The DPP note uses the convention D R(c); the two conventions are related by D(RD)D=DR and therefore have identical principal minors. Put
\[
P(c)=\frac{I+Q(c)}2.
\]
Then P is an orthogonal rank-n projection. Let d=binom(2n,n), and for each n-subset S define
\[
p_c(S)=\det P(c)_{S,S},\qquad
q_c(S)=\frac12\left[p_c(S)+p_c(S^c)\right].
\]
The complement-folded DPP identity in THEORY_PROGRAM_DPP.md says that the EUI program-label distribution is uniform exactly when
\[
\bar q(S):=\mathbb E_{c\sim\mu}q_c(S)=1/d
\quad\text{for every }|S|=n. \tag{1}
\]
The complement is bookkeeping: no claim is made that P(c) and I-P(c) are two separately implemented oracle seeds.

## Theorem 1 — exact complement-folded Plucker 1-design equivalence

Let \(\mathcal H_n=\bigwedge^n\mathbb R^{2n}\), with its standard orthonormal basis \(\{|S\rangle:|S|=n\}\). Define the two rank-one Slater projectors
\[
\rho_c^+=\bigwedge^n P(c),\qquad
\rho_c^-=\bigwedge^n(I-P(c)),
\]
and their sum
\[
\Pi_c=\rho_c^++\rho_c^-.
\]
The two determinant lines are orthogonal, so \(\Pi_c\) is a rank-two orthogonal projector. For a diagonal sign matrix
\[
S_\epsilon=\operatorname{diag}(\epsilon_0,\ldots,\epsilon_{2n-1}),
\]
define
\[
\Gamma_\epsilon=\bigwedge^n S_\epsilon,\qquad
\Pi_{c,\epsilon}=\Gamma_\epsilon\Pi_c\Gamma_\epsilon^T.
\]

The vertex-sign gauge is physically legal:
\[
c_j\longmapsto c_j^\epsilon=\epsilon_j\epsilon_{j+1}c_j,
\qquad
P(c^\epsilon)=S_\epsilon P(c)S_\epsilon.
\]
The exact equivalence is
\[
\boxed{
\bar q(S)=1/d\ \forall S
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathbb E_{c\sim\mu,\,\epsilon}
\Pi_{c,\epsilon}=\frac2d I_{\mathcal H_n},
} \tag{2}
\]
where the signs are independent and uniform. Thus the full EUI moment target is equivalent to a tight fusion-frame identity for the auxiliary rank-two Plucker subspaces generated by the physical seeds and their legal vertex-sign gauges. We call (2) the *complement-folded Plucker 1-design condition*.

### Proof

Choose an orthonormal frame U for range(P) and V for range(I-P). The unit Plucker vectors are
\[
|u\rangle=u_1\wedge\cdots\wedge u_n,\qquad
|v\rangle=v_1\wedge\cdots\wedge v_n.
\]
They are orthogonal and
\[
\rho_c^+=|u\rangle\langle u|,\qquad
\rho_c^-=|v\rangle\langle v|.
\]
The exterior-power matrix-element identity gives
\[
(\rho_c^+)_{S,T}=\det P_{S,T},\qquad
(\rho_c^-)_{S,T}=\det(I-P)_{S,T}.
\]
In particular, Jacobi's complementary-minor identity for an orthogonal projection gives
\[
(\rho_c^+)_{S,S}=p_c(S),\qquad
(\rho_c^-)_{S,S}=p_c(S^c),
\]
so \((\Pi_c)_{S,S}=2q_c(S)\).

In the Plucker basis, \(\Gamma_\epsilon|S\rangle=\chi_S(\epsilon)|S\rangle\), where
\[
\chi_S(\epsilon)=\prod_{i\in S}\epsilon_i.
\]
For two n-subsets S,T,
\[
\mathbb E_\epsilon[\chi_S(\epsilon)\chi_T(\epsilon)]
=\mathbb E_\epsilon\prod_{i\in S\triangle T}\epsilon_i
=\mathbf 1_{S=T}.
\]
Consequently the sign twirl removes every off-diagonal Plucker matrix element and preserves every diagonal one:
\[
\mathbb E_\epsilon\Pi_{c,\epsilon}
=\operatorname{diag}\bigl((\Pi_c)_{S,S}\bigr)
=2\operatorname{diag}\bigl(q_c(S)\bigr).
\]
Averaging over c proves both directions of (2). \(\square\)

## Theorem 2 — frame potential is exactly the chi-square discrepancy

For independent \(c,c'\sim\mu\) and independent uniform sign gauges \(\epsilon,\epsilon'\), define
\[
\operatorname{FP}(\mu)=
\mathbb E\operatorname{tr}(\Pi_{c,\epsilon}\Pi_{c',\epsilon'}).
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{FP}(\mu)
=4\sum_{|S|=n}\bar q(S)^2
=\frac4d\left[1+\chi^2(\bar q\Vert u)\right]
=\frac4d+4\|\bar q-u\|_2^2,
} \tag{3}
\]
where u is uniform on the d n-subsets and
\[
\chi^2(\bar q\Vert u)=d\sum_S(\bar q(S)-1/d)^2.
\]
In particular, \(\operatorname{FP}\ge4/d\), with equality if and only if the complete EUI program-mass target holds.

### Proof

Expand the trace in the Plucker basis. Independent sign averages for epsilon and epsilon-prime each kill off-diagonal terms. Therefore
\[
\mathbb E_{\epsilon,\epsilon'}
\operatorname{tr}(\Pi_{c,\epsilon}\Pi_{c',\epsilon'})
=\sum_S(\Pi_c)_{S,S}(\Pi_{c'})_{S,S}
=4\sum_S q_c(S)q_{c'}(S).
\]
Independence of c and c' gives the first equality in (3). The remaining equalities follow from \(\sum_S\bar q(S)=1\). \(\square\)

The program-label TV distance equals \(\operatorname{TV}(\bar q,u)\), because complement folding pairs S with S^c and assigns class mass 2q(S). Hence
\[
\frac14\sqrt{\operatorname{FP}-4/d}
\le \operatorname{TV}(\bar q,u)
\le \frac{\sqrt d}{4}\sqrt{\operatorname{FP}-4/d}. \tag{4}
\]
The lower bound follows from \(\|x\|_1\ge\|x\|_2\), and the upper bound from \(\|x\|_1\le\sqrt d\|x\|_2\).

## Polynomial-time overlap evaluation

Let U_c,V_c be orthonormal 2n-by-n frames for P(c) and I-P(c). For a relative diagonal sign gauge G=S_epsilon S_epsilon', the sample whose expectation is FP can be evaluated as four n-by-n determinants:
\[
\begin{aligned}
X(c,c',G)={}&|\det(U_c^T G U_{c'})|^2
+|\det(U_c^T G V_{c'})|^2\\
&+|\det(V_c^T G U_{c'})|^2
+|\det(V_c^T G V_{c'})|^2.
\end{aligned} \tag{5}
\]
This equals \(\operatorname{tr}(\Pi_{c,\epsilon}\Pi_{c',\epsilon'})\), lies in [0,2], and has expectation FP. After the two frames for each seed are available, one sample costs O(n^3) dense arithmetic and O(n^2) storage.

For M independent samples, Hoeffding gives
\[
\Pr(|\widehat{\operatorname{FP}}-\operatorname{FP}|\ge\eta)
\le 2e^{-M\eta^2/2}. \tag{6}
\]
Thus a validated one-sided lower confidence bound above 4/d rigorously rejects exact uniformity and, through (4), lower-bounds program-mass TV. A failure to reject is **not** a proof of uniformity. Since 4/d is exponentially small in n, this is a coarse falsifier when the candidate is appreciably nonuniform, not a polynomial-sample exact certification method. Numerical determinant error must be enclosed separately before using (6) as a rigorous certificate.

For N=42,
\[
d=\binom{42}{21}=538257874440,\qquad
\operatorname{FP}_{\rm target}=\frac4d
=\frac1{134564468610}.
\]
This supplies a scalar diagnostic for broad candidate ensembles without enumerating the d program labels or the 1,440 quartic orbits. It does not replace the exact all-moment certificate.

## Exact N=4 physical verification

For N=4, the three perfect matchings
\[
(01)(23),\quad (03)(12),\quad (02)(13)
\]
are all legal cycle exponentials. The first two use disjoint quarter-turn blocks; the third uses A=(pi/2)K with K^3=-4K and exp(A)=I+K^2/2. The dependency-free verifier checks these identities, all projection and Plucker-projector identities, every gauge element, and the four-determinant overlap formula for all 2,304 pairs of gauge-expanded states exactly over the rationals.

For the equal mixture of the three physical matching seeds:
- all six q(S) are exactly 1/6;
- the gauge-averaged rank-two projector is exactly I_6/3;
- the frame potential is exactly 2/3=4/6.

For one matching seed alone:
- the frame potential is 1;
- chi-square divergence is 1/2;
- program-label TV is 1/3.

This is an exact small-size verification of the reformulation and diagnostic, not an N=42 construction.

## Novelty boundary and limitations

Projection DPPs, Slater determinants, Plucker embeddings and tight fusion frames are established mathematical structures. Relevant background includes Kassel–Levy, *Determinantal probability measures on Grassmannians*, Ann. Inst. H. Poincare Comb. Phys. Interact. 9 (2022), DOI 10.4171/AIHPD/152, and the Gaussian/Slater formalism in *Matchgate Shadows for Fermionic Quantum Simulation* (Commun. Math. Phys., 2023). The EUI-specific result here is the exact complement-folded equivalence (2) for the fixed physical program and the frame-potential identity (3) as a label-free falsification statistic. No novelty is claimed for generic DPP sampling, Gaussian-state simulation, Plucker geometry or fusion-frame theory.

The complement Slater line is auxiliary bookkeeping, not a second physical oracle. The result concerns the classical program-mass distribution. It does not imply a Choi-state trace-distance or channel diamond-distance bound. No N=42 positive ensemble or global separator is obtained here.
