## Proof

For any (p\in\Delta_{143}), Hölder's inequality and the simplex constraint give
\[
\|Ap-y\|_\infty
\ge z^T(y-Ap)
=z^Ty-\sum_{j=1}^{143}p_j a_j^Tz.
\]
The Arb-certified column inequalities imply (a_j^Tz\le-\lambda+\delta) for every pinned column. Therefore
\[
-\sum_jp_ja_j^Tz\ge\lambda-\delta,
\]
and hence
\[
\|Ap-y\|_\infty\ge z^Ty+\lambda-\delta.
\]
The right-hand side is the boxed exact rational lower bound. This proves the stated finite-pool minimax obstruction.
