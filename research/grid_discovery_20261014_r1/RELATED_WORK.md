## Method changes induced by search

The geometric literature made it essential to distinguish a rank-one incidence quadric from the physical transpose quadratic refinement. The real Clifford literature then made the rank2 radical quotient the cheapest plausible falsification gate. LCU and universal-supermap papers made a second distinction essential: a rational Fourier optimum is not automatically a physical optimum. Rather than run a large physical d=8 SDP, we derived a constant-amplitude lemma and reduced the problem to d=2,4 comb certificates. All new numerical checks stay in the original physical 8×8 system, except explicitly labeled four-qubit nonembedding witnesses and logical comb certificates.

## Search limitations and negative searches

The attempted HTML URLs for older arXiv papers (including quant-ph/0304125 and 2002.05693) did not provide usable full text; the former was recovered as a PDF, the latter remains abstract-level here. A modern Clifford tensor-powers duality paper and a recent deterministic query lower-bound result were screened but not used in proofs. No claim that a search failed to find a direct match establishes originality. An independent mathematical and literature audit of the phase-defect/cubic law and the structured-family one-query classification remains appropriate.
