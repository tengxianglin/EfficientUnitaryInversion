## Branch rigidity and centring
Purify preprocessing into E:i->bM, E†E=I_i, and resolve the success instrument into Kraus contractions K_mu:aM->o with sum K_mu†K_mu<=I_aM. Define the complex-linear amplitude map L_mu(X)=K_mu(X_b->a tensor I_M)E. Exact rank-one target Choi output forces every successful branch to equal a_mu(alpha) times the target amplitude; the scalar can be extracted by Hilbert-Schmidt pairing, including at zero success.

Fix a ray H(beta), with distinct real spectral set S (multiplicities remain in the spectral projectors). Resolving output along each target spectral projector shows a_mu(t beta) is a finite exponential polynomial whose allowed frequencies lie in every S+lambda for dagger/star, or every S-lambda for transpose. Uniqueness of distinct real frequencies is valid on the complete real ray. For transpose that intersection is {0}. For dagger/star its maximum possible singleton is gamma=min(S)+max(S), present exactly when S=gamma-S. Empty intersection forces a_mu identically zero. At a nonempty intersection a_mu=c_mu exp(-it gamma). This includes the zero-at-origin case: c_mu=0 kills the entire ray. All rays share the same identity and hence the same c_mu=a_mu(0). A single asymmetric ray kills every successful branch globally.

For any nonzero dagger branch, differentiating at zero gives L_mu(H(beta))/c_mu=gamma(beta)I-H(beta). For star replace H by H^T. Taking trace isolates gamma as a complex-linear functional of real beta; its real spectral value makes it real linear. Call it ell(beta). Other surviving branches have the same spectral gamma and may have different constant complex c_mu, but cannot have distinct parameter phases. Thus success probability sum|c_mu|² is constant.

Set G=H-ell I/2 and V=exp(-iG). Linearity in the scalar exponential gives L_mu(V)=c_mu V† (or c_mu V*). Mixed partial derivatives at zero extract the whole coefficient matrices, not their coordinate supports. Comparing each degree imposes +identity on even coefficients and -identity on odd coefficients for dagger. For star compose the amplitude map with transpose to obtain the same grading. Hence intersection of the two complex coefficient spans must be zero.

Conversely disjoint spans define a unique parity involution on their sum. Extend that complex-linear map to all M_N by any fixed linear complement, using transpose afterwards for star. It obeys F(U)=exp(-i ell)U† or exp(-i ell)U* by the entire exponential series. This is only an amplitude statement until physical normalization below.
