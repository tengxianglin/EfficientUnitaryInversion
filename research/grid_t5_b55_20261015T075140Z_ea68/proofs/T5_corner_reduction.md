# T5: physical corner reduction audit

This is an analytic audit conditional on the independent dense and sparse certificates agreeing on the row/column representation. The input is an arbitrary purified, one-query physical protocol, not a numerical Kraus ansatz. Let `i,o` be the external input/output, `b,a` the oracle input/output, and `m` all finite coherent memory. Its pre-isometry is `E:i -> b m`, `E†E=I_i`; successful post-Kraus operators are `K_mu:a m -> o`, with `sum_mu K_mu†K_mu <= I_am`. One unconditional forward oracle call gives the amplitude map `L_mu(X)=K_mu(X_b->a tensor I_m)E`. A successful exact inverse CP map has rank-one Choi output, so each branch separately has `L_mu(U(beta))=a_mu(beta)U(beta)†` (including a zero branch). No controlled oracle or common branch phase is assumed.

The independently certified decomposition is a rank-two `++` centre plus a rank-six corner. In each sector the Hamiltonian is traceless on its first-qubit block, so every ray has a symmetric spectrum. For a ray with distinct spectral set `S`, frequency comparison of `L_mu(exp(-itH))` and `a_mu(t)exp(+itH)` restricts the scalar frequencies to `intersection_{lambda in S}(S+lambda)={0}`. Thus `a_mu(t)=c_mu`, and all rays share `U(0)=I`, fixing `c_mu=a_mu(0)` globally. A zero-at-origin branch vanishes on every ray. Each nonzero branch has constant success amplitude and `p=sum_mu |c_mu|^2` on the full physical promise.

The saved complete whole-coefficient bases, independently checked against the literal dense representation, have even span `span{Pi_0,Pi_c}` and odd span `span{Pi_0(XII+YII+ZII),B_x,B_y,B_z}`. Comparing every multivariate Taylor coefficient of `L_mu(U(beta))=c_mu U(beta)†` yields `L_mu(X_even)=c_mu X_even` and `L_mu(X_odd)=-c_mu X_odd`. The four corner units are in their sum, and `J(e_jk tensor I_3)J†=M_jk`. On their basis this gives, for every `X in M_2`, 

`L_mu(J(X tensor I_3)J†)=c_mu J((Tr(X)I_2-X) tensor I_3)J†`.  (1)

This is a linear-amplitude statement on nonunitary corner elements. It does not claim that `J(X tensor I_3)J†` itself is a promised physical eight-dimensional oracle.

Let the deterministic `J:C^2 tensor C^3 -> C^8` obey `J†J=I_6` and `JJ†=Pi_c`. Define `E'=(J† tensor I_m)EJ:C^6 -> C^6 tensor m` and `K'_mu=J†K_mu(J tensor I_m):C^6 tensor m -> C^6`. Compression gives `E'†E' <= I_6` and `sum_mu K'_mu†K'_mu <= I_(6m)`. Directly retaining the middle projector, for any `V in SU(2)`,

`K'_mu((V tensor I_3) tensor I_m)E' = J† L_mu(J(V tensor I_3)J†)J = c_mu(V† tensor I_3)`.  (2)

The pre-map `E'` is a contraction, not generally an isometry. Complete it with a failure/reject flag using `(I_6-E'†E')^(1/2)`. The reject branch still presents a dummy two-dimensional wire to the same unconditional `V` query and is never labeled successful. Complete the successful post-instrument with failure Kraus operators using the positive remainder `I_(6m)-sum K'_mu†K'_mu`. Both completions are parameter independent; the flags may be kept in coherent memory until measurement. Equation (2) is the **unnormalized** successful amplitude, so completion neither divides by an acceptance probability nor alters any `c_mu`. The multiplicity `C^3` is maintained coherently throughout (2). Feeding a fixed known multiplicity state and extracting it at the output gives a legitimate universal qubit inverse protocol with the same `p`; its successful output is always the same spectator, so extracting it introduces no loss. The original eight-dimensional construction remains full and coherent across all four centres.

For `V in SU(2)`, `V†=A_2(V)=Tr(V)I_2-V=Y V^T Y`; `SU(2)` spans `M_2`. The received exact T2 certificate in `evidence/cycle/results/T2_final/comb_index_audit.json` uses tensor order `(i,b,a,o)` and checks all four matrix-unit images of `A_2`, `Q=Tr_o |t><t|`, `Q^2=dQ`, `Tr_a Q=I_ib`, and `Tr(Q^2)=d^3` at `d=2`; see also `evidence/cycle/proofs/one_query_audit.md`, §5. Its normalized deterministic completion obeys `C_det >= p|t><t|`, `Tr_o C_det=C_1 tensor I_a`, and `Tr_b C_1=I_i`. Taking the trace against `Q` gives `d=Tr[(C_1 tensor I_a)Q] >= p Tr(Q^2)=p d^3`, hence `p<=1/d^2=1/4`. This known bound applies here only because the explicit physical contraction completion produced a universal qubit protocol with the same unnormalized success amplitude.

The full-grid coherent LCU implementation attains `p=1/4`: `E_LCU|psi>=(1/2)sum_j |j> sigma_j|psi>`, `K_LCU(|j> tensor |phi>)=(s_j/2)sigma_j|phi>`, where `sigma=(I,XII,YII,ZII)` and `s=(-,+,+,+)`. `E_LCU†E_LCU=I_8`, `K_LCU K_LCU†=I_8`, and `K_LCU(I_4 tensor U)E_LCU=U†/2` by the parity action on the complete coefficient span. This acts on the original physical oracle, so centre superpositions are preserved. The separate known `Q0=YIX` sandwiches yield star and transpose. No ancilla or gate optimality follows.
