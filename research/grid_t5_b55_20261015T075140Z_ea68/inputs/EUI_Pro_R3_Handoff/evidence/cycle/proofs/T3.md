# T3: explicit target-aware transport and primitive circuit construction

Status: **completed, numerical linear certificates and finite-support circuit corroboration**. Inputs are the immutable r1 `embedding1024.json` and `all_anti_grids.json`. This audit does not supply an independent probability-optimality proof and makes no claim about the fewest ancillas or known gates.

## Explicit representation encoding

`code/audit_transport.py` selects exactly the 37 physical quadratic orbit representatives listed by the input. There are 19 rank-two and 18 rank-four representatives. It implements binary symplectic Gram–Schmidt directly, independently of the r1 program. For each representative it returns symplectic pairs `(x_j,z_j)` and a radical basis `r_l` in the original four-generator coordinates. Physical Pauli strings are obtained from the stored physical seed basis, without dropping signs during parameter reconstruction.

Let `X_j=P(x_j)`, `Z_j=P(z_j)`, `R_l=P(r_l)`, all standard Hermitian Paulis. For every radical eigenvalue vector `s`, construct

\[
\Pi_s=\prod_l\frac{I+(-1)^{s_l}R_l}{2},\qquad
E_{s,0}=\Pi_s\prod_j\frac{I+Z_j}{2}.
\]

The algorithm projects computational coordinate vectors through `E_{s,0}`, orthonormalizes the nonzero columns and fixes their first nonzero component to a positive real number. These columns solve the Pauli eigen-equations. Applying products of the `X_j` gives the complete isometry `E_s`. Concatenating all `E_s` gives the physical encoding unitary `C`. The order is `(center sector, logical basis, multiplicity)`.

For rank two, all four center sectors occur, each with logical dimension two and multiplicity one. For rank four, there is one center sector, logical dimension four and multiplicity two. The second multiplicity basis vector is constructed and retained: the representation is explicitly `M4 tensor I2`, not a smaller free oracle. Every original Pauli is reconstructed from its signed logical Pauli in every sector. The output includes all 16 label maps and the nine row-major parameter sign maps. No center measurement, dephasing, or spectator discard occurs.

The raw files save `C`, `Cdagger`, the target input/output encodings, every center projector and sector isometry, and all algebra matrix units. For rank two these are `E_s e_ij E_sdagger`; for rank four they are `E (e_ij tensor I2) Edagger`. All 592 algebra units across the 37 representatives were checked for encoding, trace, the three target identities and all pairwise multiplication identities. Another 2,368 full physical input matrix units were checked for round-trip encoding and all three target rules; these include off-diagonal center and multiplicity coherences and are not claimed to be oracle-algebra elements. The maximum observed residual is **6.682592795753133e-16**, below the preregistered `1e-10` tolerance, using `complex128` arithmetic. The largest matrix in this encoding task is 8 by 8. Each representative also passes exact binary checks of all 16 physical quadratic values and all 256 physical commutator pairs against the supplied `(B,q)`.

## Target encoding and center permutation

With `U=C V Cdagger`, the correct target rules are

\[
U^T=C^*V^TC^T,\qquad U^*=C^*V^*C^T,\qquad
U^\dagger=C V^\dagger C^\dagger.
\]

The program checks these rules separately on every algebra matrix unit. For each radical basis operator,

\[
R_l^T=(-1)^{q(r_l)}R_l,
\quad \Pi_s^T=\Pi_{s\mathbin\oplus q_R}.
\]

Every rank-two representative therefore includes an explicit four-sector permutation, verified on the physical projectors. Thirteen of the 19 rank-two representatives have a nontrivial permutation. Its target encoding remains coherent even when this permutation is nontrivial. Neither logical rank nor a sector count by itself establishes this target transformation.

The cheapest false-transfer control is performed before the encoding audit. For `C=S=diag(1,i)` and `U=exp(-0.37 i X)`, the naive transpose expression `C U^T Cdagger` has Frobenius residual **1.022802896496509**. The corrected `C* U^T C^T` residual is **0**. This is an intentionally failing control, not a failed positive gate.

## Actual ordered circuits for every all-anti grid

`code/compile_all_anti.py` takes all 30 input examples. Put `A=P_a`, `D=P_b`, using the first two entries of the first row. They anticommute. For ancilla bits `(a,b)`, define

\[
\mathrm{SELECT}=\sum_{a,b}|a,b\rangle\langle a,b|\otimes A^aD^b.
\]

The ordered unitary circuit is: Hadamards on both ancillas; `SELECTdagger`; one unmodified forward physical `U`; `SELECT`; the ancilla reflection `diag(-1,1,1,1)`; Hadamards; postselect `00`. The reflection is exactly `X0,X1,CZ01,X1,X0` in time order. This choice keeps its absolute sign and does not silently discard a global phase. `SELECTdagger` is time-ordered controlled-A then controlled-D; `SELECT` is controlled-D then controlled-A.

Each controlled Pauli factor is compiled into one/two-qubit primitives. Controlled X and Z are CX and CZ. Controlled Y is the explicit time-ordered sequence `Sdag(target), CX(control,target), S(target)`, because `S X Sdag=Y`. There are no hidden multi-controlled known operations. The success block is

\[
K=\frac{-U+AUA+DUD+(AD)U(DA)}4=\frac{U^\dagger}{2}.
\]

The last equality holds in every traceless two-dimensional center block, via the SU(2) adjugate identity; the input whole-grid family has exactly these blocks. The circuit therefore has probability `1/4`, with two clean ancillas and total workspace five. The star circuit is the time-ordered physical Pauli `Q0`, forward `U`, physical Pauli `Q0`, using the supplied witness. Its generator equations `Q0 P_g Q0=-P_g*` are verified separately for every one of the nine independent terms, giving `Q0 U Q0=U*`. Star has probability one, no extra ancilla and workspace three. Both constructions have `K=P=Q=1` under the original physical eight-dimensional forward-oracle contract; neither uses controlled-U.

The emitted ordered gate lists give the actual known primitive counts of this construction. Dagger uses **8–16 one-qubit gates and 5–7 two-qubit gates** on the 30 supplied physical representatives. Star uses **2–4 one-qubit gates and zero two-qubit gates**. Dagger's two-qubit count is exactly `2(weight(A)+weight(D))+1`; its one-qubit count includes the four reflection X gates, four preparation/unpreparation H gates and every S/Sdag in controlled-Y decomposition. These are all-to-all construction counts, without a device topology. No hardware SWAP routing cost or synthesis optimum is claimed.
