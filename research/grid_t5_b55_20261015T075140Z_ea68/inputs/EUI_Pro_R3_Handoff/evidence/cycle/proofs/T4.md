## Exact method and stopping argument

Every row-major physical Pauli is a standard Hermitian 8×8 matrix. Coordinates are the64 Pauli strings, base4 alphabet I,X,Y,Z, leftmost qubit first. Single-letter products preserve XY=iZ, YZ=iX, ZX=iY and reversed negative phases. The implementation propagates every commutative beta monomial in H^k using Python integer real/imaginary pairs. Each whole monomial coefficient is Hermitian, and its imaginary Pauli coefficients cancel exactly. Vectors, not union supports, enter independent rational row elimination.

For any fixed beta, Cayley–Hamilton expresses H^k, k>=8, in the span of I,H,...,H^7 with scalar polynomial functions of beta. Equating polynomial coefficients shows that every higher coefficient vector lies in the constant linear span of coefficient vectors through degree7. Together with the mixed-derivative/exponential argument in T2, this gives the actual full exponential span. This argument does not close either parity separately. Applying Cayley–Hamilton instead to H² closes even powers through H14 and odd powers through H15. The tied families have only1 or3 variables, and every coefficient at all these degrees is saved. Independent9-variable controls are computed through degree3 only; their full-span stopping premise is T1, and no independent-family graded closure is inferred.

A second implementation rebuilds physical Pauli matrices from literal 2×2 matrices. SymPy DomainMatrix rational ranks independently confirm all full/even/odd ranks, and dense exact matrix powers at two fixed integer beta vectors per family confirm416 saved polynomial evaluations. These evaluations corroborate the recurrence; completeness comes from all monomial coefficients plus the separate Cayley–Hamilton argument. The transpose restriction uses actual signs (-1)^number_of_Y and augmented ranks. Non-invariance means T(E) is outside E; it does not mean transpose is physically impossible.

## Fixed-map diagnostics and what remains open

The even/odd coefficient constraints for fixed-amplitude dagger are L(v)=+v on even and L(v)=-v on odd; star additionally applies coordinate transpose signs. Exact augmented ranks test consistency. The first9 tied families have nonzero even/odd intersection and fail this diagnostic. The3 B55 families have disjoint even/odd spaces and admit the saved fixed linear maps. This supplies no complete-positivity or query-comb realization by itself.

For B55, physical existence is already inherited from the enclosing independent family once T2/T3 pass: star p1 and dagger p1/4 remain valid on every tied subfamily. Star p1 is automatically optimal. Their tied-family dagger or transpose optimal probabilities and minimum resources are not settled here: the full-algebra lower-bound reduction need not survive span dimension6 or4. Any proposed improvement needs a physical construction and an upper bound respecting parameter-dependent branch phases and all coherent center sectors. Do not mislabel known inherited existence as unresolved, and do not transfer the independent family's upper bound to a smaller promise without a new reduction.
