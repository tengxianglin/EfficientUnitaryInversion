## Independent route and exact coverage

The C++ backend enumerates reduced-row-echelon bases of all four-dimensional
subspaces of F2^(2n), then independently enumerates all 20,160 ordered bases
of F2^4. It does not traverse four nested physical-generator choices and
does not import, copy, or call any r1 mathematical helper. For each physical
basis, q0 is a direct x dot z parity and omega is the direct symplectic dot
product. Histograms use type ID `16*Bmask+qmask`, with B bits ordered
01,02,03,12,13,23. Integer physical vectors place x in the low n bits, z in
the next n bits; printable Pauli words put qubit zero first.

## Embedding argument and cheapest kill

A rank-zero four-space would be symplectically isotropic of dimension four
inside a six-dimensional nondegenerate symplectic space, which is
impossible. For rank four, adjoining a nondegenerate quadratic plane of
the same Arf invariant gives a six-dimensional plus-type space, so an
embedding exists.

For rank two with singular radical R, an embedding must have W=R-perp:
both have dimension four. Quotienting the ambient hyperbolic six-space by
the two singular radical directions leaves a hyperbolic plane; an
anisotropic quotient is therefore impossible. Conversely a hyperbolic
plane plus two singular radical directions embeds directly. Its Gauss
sum on W is +8, whereas the prohibited quotient gives -8.

If q on R is nonzero, choose radical basis r1,r2 with q(r1)=1,q(r2)=0.
Adding r1 to either nondegenerate basis vector changes its q value while
preserving all pairings, leaving a single such isometry type. A model is
R=span{YII,IXI}, with nondegenerate complement span{IIX,IIZ}.

Thus all 28 rank-four B types admit all 16 q choices, and all 35 rank-two
B types admit 15 choices. The exact census gives 973 types and 51 forbidden
types: 16 rank-zero and 35 rank-two-minus. The old rank-only count 1008
retains precisely those 35 false positives.

For Bmask=1,qmask=3 the radical is {0,4,8,12}, every radical vector has
q=0, and the Gauss sum is -8. The complete n3 census finds count zero.
Both an independent four-qubit lift and the explicit supplied control
grid have binary seed rank four and bilinear rank two, B=1,q=3, and pass
all six-line incidence checks:

```
YIII  XYII  ZYII
IIZI  IIIZ  IIZZ
YIZI  XYIZ  ZYZZ
```

## Direct H2/H3 arithmetic and all-order consequence

For each of the 64 B masks, the audit constructs an explicit four-qubit
lift with independent x labels. Products use an explicit single-letter
I,X,Y,Z multiplication table. For every degree-two and degree-three
commutative monomial, it adds **all distinct orderings**, keeping exact
Gaussian-integer coefficients; it does not infer coefficients from a
previous support table or from Jordan closure. This produces 45 H2 and
165 H3 coefficients per mask, including cancellations and repeated-index
monomials. All imaginary components vanish exactly.

With grid labels G={1,2,3,4,8,12,5,10,15}, geometric form
Delta(v)=v0*v3+v1*v2 and polar Jmask=12, each Delta-one point has exactly
three decompositions into two distinct grid points. The six participating
points span F2^4 in every case; all decompositions are saved. A pair's
symmetrized coefficient vanishes exactly when its two Pauli factors
anticommute. Consequently the missing H2 labels are exactly

`{x: Delta(x)=1 and (B+J)(x,e_i)=0 for every i}`.

The direct coefficients verify this equality for every B. There are 34
types already saturated through H2, 29 requiring H3, and the special
B=J type with dimension ten. B=4 retains the H2 counterexample missing
label nine; its (2,4,15) cubic coefficient is +6 in the chosen lift.

For B unequal to J, a missing x belongs to the radical of nonzero C=B+J.
As C is alternating and singular, it has rank two. The independently
constructed 72-element incidence group is transitive on the six
Delta-one points, so x can be sent to nine. The seven nonzero C masks
having nine in their radical are independently enumerated, and explicit
fully symmetrized cubic coefficients are recomputed:

| C | B=C+J | Triple | Coefficient |
|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 8 | 4 | 2,4,15 | 6 |
| 17 | 29 | 1,2,10 | -2 |
| 25 | 21 | 1,2,10 | -2 |
| 34 | 46 | 1,4,12 | -2 |
| 42 | 38 | 1,4,12 | -2 |
| 51 | 63 | 1,2,10 | -2 |
| 59 | 55 | 1,2,10 | -2 |

The signs depend on the lift; nonvanishing does not. On each triple the
C-edge parity and J-edge parity are both odd, so the number of
anticommuting B edges is zero or two, giving a nonzero symmetrized Pauli
coefficient of magnitude six or two. Transport therefore supplies every
H2-missing point at H3.

For B=J, the linear operation tau(P_v)=(-1)^Delta(v) P_v reverses products:
the quadratic polarization identity supplies exactly the sign needed
when swapping two Pauli factors. Tau fixes each grid generator and hence
every H^k, so no Delta-one coordinate can occur at any degree. The ten
coordinates I and G are already independent. For B unequal to J the
degree-three span contains all sixteen coordinates and is the entire
associative Pauli algebra, so higher powers cannot add coordinates.

These are statements about **proper grids with nine independent real
coefficients**. Coefficient binding can create linear combinations in a
single monomial coefficient, so union support is no longer a span-rank
certificate. Nothing here promotes tied families to the same theorem.

## Phase, full Gram and orbit reconstruction

The six ordered line products are evaluated from the single-letter
table, yielding their actual powers of i. Their product is exactly
(-1)^(p+t+u+w+z) in all 64 four-qubit lifts. The observed line parity gives
p,z,t,w and u+v; the product phase supplies u, then v. The reconstructed
9-by-9 Gram matrix agrees entrywise with the direct physical x,z Gram for
all masks. No orbit representative was used to filter these 64 inputs.

All 1024 abstract quadratic types have distinct nine-q-plus-phase
signatures. The incidence group is built directly by testing independent
images of the four seeds that preserve the nine-point grid. Its 72
actions pass identity, inverse, closure and Delta-one transitivity tests.
The canonicalizer is invariant under all 72 actions on each of the 1024
types (73,728 tests). It gives 44 abstract quadratic orbits, 37 physical
n3 quadratic orbits, and eight physical B-only orbits. The n3 acceptance
predicate is constant on every quadratic orbit.

## Resource accounting and limits

All compilation and computation used the shared serial bounded runner,
one pinned CPU, RAM cap 1024 MiB and GPU=0. No package installation,
network scientific computation, GPU run or solver occurred. The four
recorded T1 runs (compile plus three fresh audited outputs) consumed
6.583309 CPU seconds. Source-provenance reconstruction adds 0.024404 CPU
seconds, for a total 6.607713 of the 480 CPU-second T1 cap.

No floating-point scientific result is used in T1: all empirical checks
are exact-finite integer calculations. The analytic extension depends on
the stated finite quadratic-space and anti-automorphism arguments, which
are made explicit above. General coefficient subspaces, nonproper grids,
physical circuit costs, and novelty remain outside this audit.
