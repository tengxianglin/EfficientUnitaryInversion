# Escape of the full-fourth separator by a general gapped open path (n=21)

Date: 2026-11-24. Related: Issue #5.

## Result

The 80-term rational separator that is rigorously positive on the 6,123-column fractional single-path grid and on the entire one-parameter single-open-path continuum is **not** a separator for the full gapped nearest-neighbour family.

A deterministic pricing search found both:

1. a connected open path with all 41 interior couplings strictly positive; and
2. a four-segment open path with three additional exact zero edges.

The connected candidate alone is enough to refute extension of this particular separator to all gapped paths. Its 41 positive couplings are frozen as finite decimal rationals in \`evidence/ising_full_fourth_general_gapped_search_n21.json\`. The wrap coupling is exactly zero.

\]

while for the connected candidate

\[

\[
y^{\mathsf T}b-y^{\mathsf T}a(c)

This is a strict, large-margin counterexample to the **global use of this frozen dual**, not a counterexample to the physical model or a proof that the target moment vector is feasible.

The independent verifier also checks all 1,764 entries of \(R(c)^{\mathsf T}R(c)-I\) by Arb intervals containing zero and certifies \(\det R(c)=1\). The candidate therefore gives an explicitly certified element of \(SO(42)\) generated by the required skew-symmetric nearest-neighbour Hamiltonian.

## Exact physical family and sign-gauge reduction

For \(c=(c_0,\ldots,c_{40},0)\), define

\[
A(c)_{j,j+1}=2c_j,\qquad
A(c)_{j+1,j}=-2c_j,\qquad j=0,\ldots,40,
\]

with the wrap edge \(c_{41}=0\), and \(R(c)=e^{A(c)}\).

It is sufficient to search \(c_j\ge0\). Given arbitrary real couplings, choose \(d_0=1\) and recursively choose \(d_{j+1}=d_j\operatorname{sgn}(c_j)\) whenever \(c_j\ne0\). For \(D=\operatorname{diag}(d_0,\ldots,d_{41})\),

\[
A(|c|)=D A(c)D,\qquad R(|c|)=D R(c)D.
\]

Every principal minor is invariant under this diagonal sign conjugation because

\[
\det (D R D)_{I,I}=\det(D_I)^2\det R_{I,I}=\det R_{I,I}.
\]

The distance second-moment coordinate \(R_{ii}R_{jj}-R_{ij}R_{ji}\) is invariant as well. Thus nonnegative couplings cover all sign patterns in this open-path family.

The connected witness has

\[
0<c_j\le17.278924606002036,\qquad
\min_j c_j=10^{-6},\qquad j=0,\ldots,40,
\]

and \(c_{41}=0\). No interior edge is zero. The multi-segment witness has internal zero edges \(j=6,24,36\), in addition to the wrap cut.
