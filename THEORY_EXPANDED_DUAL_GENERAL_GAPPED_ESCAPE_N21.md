# Certified escape from the expanded full-fourth separator (n=21)

Date: 2026-11-24. Related: Issue #5.

## Result

The exact rational separator certified on the finite pool of 6,124 columns is **not** a valid separator for the full connected gapped open-path family.

A deterministic pricing experiment produced a 42-site open-path generator with

\[
c_{41}=0,\qquad c_j>0\quad(j=0,\ldots,40),
\]

where all 41 interior couplings are frozen as exact decimal rationals in
`evidence/ising_full_fourth_plus_general_gapped_search_n21.json`.
The minimum interior coupling is
\[
\min_{0\le j\le40}c_j=9.9999999999999995\times10^{-7}>0,
\]
and the maximum is
\[
\max_j c_j=25.192470329143369.
\]
Thus this is a connected open path, not a path with an internal cut. The exact rational vector is also copied into
`evidence/ising_full_fourth_plus_general_gapped_escape_arb_n21.json`.

For the 75-term rational separator from the 6,124-column finite-pool certificate, the 256-bit Arb verifier gives

\[
y^{\mathsf T}b\in
[0.3989655546267208712889597414925636020952715744146046618572\ldots],
\]

\[
y^{\mathsf T}F(R(c))\in
[41.9015085570049119787721158205749180347396187452542272\ldots],
\]

and hence

\[
\boxed{
y^{\mathsf T}(b-F(R(c)))
\in[-41.5025430023781911074831560790823544326443471708396225\ldots]<0.
}
\]

The sign is separated from zero by an enormous interval margin. The independent verifier also checks all 1,764 entries of
\(R(c)^{\mathsf T}R(c)-I\) with Arb intervals containing zero and certifies
\(\det R(c)=1\). The candidate is therefore an explicitly certified element of
\(SO(42)\) generated by the prescribed real skew nearest-neighbour open-path Hamiltonian.

A second, two-segment candidate with an internal zero edge at 28 also escapes this separator, but it is not needed for the conclusion.

## What this does and does not prove

**Proved:** the particular rational dual that separates the 6,124 listed columns cannot be extended unchanged to all connected nonuniform gapped open paths. The new path is itself strictly connected, so restricting the physical family to positive interior couplings does not rescue that dual.

**Still valid:** the 6,124-column finite-pool theorem remains correct for exactly its pinned columns. A point outside that finite pool does not invalidate a theorem whose quantifier is explicitly restricted to that pool.

**Not proved:** feasibility or infeasibility of the full 1,440-orbit second-/fourth-order target over the continuous gapped family; existence of a positive all-even-principal-minor ensemble; or any Choi-state / channel diamond-distance bound.

The optimizer is only a discovery mechanism. Its best connected run reached the iteration limit, but this does not affect the mathematical witness: the couplings were frozen as exact rationals and independently certified by Arb.
