With D_m:C_m -> C_{m-1} the down operator and D_m^T its adjoint, direct counting gives
[
D_{k+1}D_{k+1}^T=D_k^TD_k+(N-2k)I.
]
Exact pair matching implies D_4 delta_4=0. Balanced-sign identities give
[
D_6 delta_6=-D_5^T delta_4.
]
Putting b=D_5^T delta_4, the commutator identity yields
[
D_6D_6^Tb=(2N-18)b,
qquad
||b||^2=(N-8)||delta_4||^2.
]

[
delta_6^{harm}=delta_6+(1/(2N-18))D_6^TD_5^Tdelta_4

[
||delta_6^{harm}||^2=||delta_6||^2-((N-8)/(2N-18))||delta_4||^2.
]
For N=42 the correction factor is 17/33. This gives an exact formula for the full sixth-harmonic projection without a dense pseudoinverse.
