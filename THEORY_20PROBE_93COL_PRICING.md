# 93-column dual pricing protocol

The 20-probe 128-bit Arb certificate proves a lower bound for mixtures of the exact 93 active physical columns. The next column-generation step evaluates the same rational dual against all 1,641 canonical legal path generators.

- The binary64 script screens all 1,548 unused columns and ranks their reduced-cost scores.
- The 128-bit Arb script independently reconstructs the exact normalized moments for the ten highest positive candidates.
- A positive Arb interval means that candidate violates the current dual column inequality and may enter a new expanded pool.
- A nonpositive screen is not a proof that all unused columns satisfy the inequality; only Arb intervals are certificates.

The scripts pin the 20-probe LP, 93-column dual certificate, frozen seed pool, and canonical full-column hash. They do not change the fixed-93-column theorem's scope.
