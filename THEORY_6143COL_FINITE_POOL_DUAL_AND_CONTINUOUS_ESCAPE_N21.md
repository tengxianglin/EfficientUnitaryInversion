## 4. What does and does not follow

The two escape witnesses do **not** show that the balanced target belongs to the convex hull of the full physical family. They only disprove this particular dual as a global separator. A different dual may exist, or a positive ensemble may exist; neither has been established.

The finite-pool theorem in Section 2 remains valid because its candidate set and hash-pinned certificate are unchanged. The two escape paths have been appended to form a 6,145-column pool.
